A visible exchange spread is only the gross starting point. The useful number is what remains after every cost required to execute the trade.

The complete arbitrage cost stack

  • Buy trading fee: charged when entering the position.
  • Sell trading fee: charged on the destination venue.
  • Withdrawal or network fee: relevant when transferring assets between venues.
  • Slippage: the difference between the displayed price and your actual average fill.
  • Execution risk: the spread can change before both sides complete.

Simple net-profit formula

Net result ≈ gross spread value − buy fee − sell fee − transfer costs − slippage.
