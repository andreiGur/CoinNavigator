How to verify a ETH spread

  1. Open the live scanner and compare the available ETH quotes.
  2. Check order-book depth and whether both exchanges support the relevant network and withdrawals.
  3. Estimate buy and sell trading fees, withdrawal/network costs, slippage and transfer delay.
  4. Only consider a route after confirming both executable prices and your risk limits.

Why a large spread may not be profitable

Fees, thin liquidity, deposit holds, transfer time, exchange restrictions and sudden price changes can erase an apparent opportunity. The scanner is informational, not financial advice.
