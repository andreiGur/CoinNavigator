How Bitcoin arbitrage works

A BTC arbitrage setup compares the same Bitcoin market across two venues: buy where the executable price is lower and sell where it is higher. The gap must exceed the total cost of the trade.

Before trading a BTC spread
  • Check current BTC order-book depth on both exchanges.
  • Include both trading fees.
  • Check Bitcoin withdrawal fees and deposit/withdrawal status if transferring BTC.
  • Account for slippage at your intended size.
  • Recheck the spread immediately before execution.
