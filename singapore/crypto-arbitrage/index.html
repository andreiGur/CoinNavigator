Singapore users: verify the venue first

The Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) maintains the official Financial Institutions Directory. Its Digital Payment Token Service filter currently lists licensed providers, including Coinbase Singapore, OKX SG, Independent Reserve SG and SBI Coinhako Technology.

How to use CoinNavigator from Singapore

1. Scan

Open the live scanner and find price differences for the same USDT pair across tracked exchanges.

2. Verify

A gross spread is not profit. Check trading fees, withdrawal costs, network availability, liquidity and slippage.

3. Check access

Before opening or funding an account, confirm that the provider and product are available to you in Singapore and check its current regulatory status.

Why a large spread can disappear

Arbitrage opportunities can shrink while assets are transferred. Trading fees apply on both sides, withdrawal/network costs can be material, and thin order books can produce slippage. CoinNavigator's scanner is discovery tooling; use the profit calculator and live verification tools before treating a spread as actionable.

Important: CoinNavigator is an informational tool, not financial advice and not a statement that a particular exchange or crypto product is suitable or legally available to you. Regulatory status and product availability can change. Verify directly with MAS and the provider.
