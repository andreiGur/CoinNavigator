Crypto arbitrage scanner FAQ

What is a crypto arbitrage scanner?

It compares prices for the same crypto asset across exchanges and surfaces differences that may be worth investigating.

Does a positive spread mean I can make that percentage?

No. Trading fees, transfer costs, slippage, liquidity and execution delay all matter. Always calculate the net result.

Do I need to connect my wallet or API keys?

No for CoinNavigator's market scanner. It uses public market data for discovery.
